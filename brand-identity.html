TikozeSOU FITNESS

Tikoze means a friendly chat in Kreyòl. Tikoze Sou Fitness is a conversation about fitness: where Haitian sport came from, how to eat our food and stay healthy, and personal coaching from someone who shares the culture. The brand is proud, direct and warm, like talking training with a friend on the gallery. It is dressed in black and orange-yellow, with the energy of a gym after dark.

01

The logo

A speech bubble for tikoze, in orange-yellow, with a black dumbbell across its centre. One mark that says conversation and training.

  1. Speech bubble. Tikoze, the conversation. The tail points down-left, toward the reader.
  2. Orange-yellow. The brand accent fills the whole bubble so the mark reads at any size.
  3. Dumbbell. Black, sitting on the centre line of the bubble.
  4. Wordmark. Tikoze in Saira ExtraBold, SOU FITNESS in wide-spaced capitals underneath.

04

Pattern & graphics

The slanted stripe is the brand's signature. Use it as a divider under headings, and as a bold pattern on posters and merch.

Stripe divider. Six slanted bars, under every section heading.

Bold stripe. Event posters and merch only.

Plain card. Article covers when a photo is missing.

Photography: real people training in Haiti and the diaspora, shot with low light and deep shadows, or in black and white. Use archive photos for history stories, always with a credit line.

05

Voice

We talk like a coach who is also family: encouraging, direct, and proud of where we come from. English first, with Kreyòl where it feels natural.

We say

  • "Keep your diri ak pwa. Change the portion."
  • "Cator jumped 7.93 m in 1928. What's your next step?"
  • Short sentences, real numbers, real names.

We don't say

  • "Cut out Haitian food to lose weight."
  • Shame, before-and-after pressure, crash diets.
  • History claims without a source.
